EXECUTION TRACE
    01 / METHOD

    Reach. Move. Settle.

    Numerical inverse kinematics checks the target. Torque-limited actuators drive the transition under gravity. Actual solver forces are checked against grip ratings and applicable friction cones.

    02 / SCOPE

    A model, with boundaries.

    Controlled reaches on synthetic routes. Grip and actuator parameters are illustrative. No dynamic catches, deformable fingers, or body–wall collision. A controller failure is not proof that a human move is impossible.
